## 蟆主・驕守ｨ・

$$\begin{align*}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\frac{x^2+y^2}{2\sigma^2}} e^{-j2\pi(ux+vy)} dx dy \\
{}={} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \exp\!{\left(-\frac{x^2}{2\sigma^2}-j2\pi ux\right)} dx
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \exp\!{\left(-\frac{y^2}{2\sigma^2}-j2\pi vy\right)} dy
  .
\end{align*}$$

蟾ｦ縺ｮ陲ｫ遨榊・髢｢謨ｰ縺ｮ謖・焚繧・f(x, u)$縺ｨ鄂ｮ縺・※蟷ｳ譁ｹ螳梧・縺吶ｋ縺ｨ莉･荳九・繧医≧縺ｫ縺ｪ繧九・
$$\begin{align*}
f(x, u) := {} -\frac{x^2}{2\sigma^2} - j2\pi ux \\
{}={} -\frac{1}{2\sigma^2} (x^2 + j4\pi\sigma^2 ux) \\
{}={} -\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\!\left( (x+j2\sigma^2\pi u)^2+(2\pi\sigma^2)^2u^2 \right)\!.
\end{align*}$$

縺薙ｌ繧堤畑縺・※陲ｫ遨榊・髢｢謨ｰ$\exp(f(x, u))$縺ｯ莉･荳九・繧医≧縺ｫ謨ｴ逅・〒縺阪ｋ縲・

\exp(f(x, u)) = {} \exp\!{\left( -\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\!\left( (x+j2\sigma^2\pi u)^2+(2\pi\sigma^2)^2u^2 \right) \right)} \\
{}={} \exp\!{\left( -\frac{1}{2\sigma^2} (x+j2\sigma^2\pi u)^2 \right)} \exp{(-2\pi^2\sigma^2 u^2)} .
\end{align*}$$

蜿ｳ縺ｮ謖・焚髢｢謨ｰ縺ｯ縺昴・謖・焚縺ｫ遨榊・螟画焚$x$繧貞性縺ｾ縺ｪ縺・°繧牙ｮ壽焚縺ｨ縺ｿ縺ｪ縺帙ｋ縺薙→縺ｫ豕ｨ諢上＠縺ｦ縲√ぎ繧ｦ繧ｹ遨榊・縺ｮ邨先棡繧剃ｽｿ縺医・莉･荳九・繧医≧縺ｫ遨榊・縺ｧ縺阪ｋ縲・

{}={} \exp{(-2\pi^2\sigma^2 u^2)} \sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2} .
\end{align*}$$

縺励◆縺後▲縺ｦ莉･荳九・騾壹ｊ隗｣縺悟ｾ励ｉ繧後ｋ縲・

  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \exp\!{\left(f(x, u) \right)} \, dx
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \exp\!{\left(f(y, v) \right)} \, dy \\
{}={} \frac{1}{2\pi\sigma^2}
  \cdot \exp{(-2\pi^2\sigma^2 u^2)} \sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2} 
  \cdot \exp{(-2\pi^2\sigma^2 v^2)} \sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2} \\
{}={} \exp\!{\left( -2 \pi^2 \sigma^2 (u^2 + v^2) \right)} .
\end{align*}$$
