This is an inline expression: $y = ax$.

For $a \neq 0$, the following proposition holds.

$$
a x^2 + bx + c = 0 \implies x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}.
$$

Some examples used `align*` environment.

$$
\begin{align*}
f(x, u) & := -\frac{x^2}{2\sigma^2} - j2\pi ux \\
& {} = -\frac{1}{2\sigma^2} (x^2 + j4\pi\sigma^2 ux) \\
& {} = -\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\!\left( (x+j2\sigma^2\pi u)^2+(2\pi\sigma^2)^2u^2 \right)\!,
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\begin{cases}
(x + y)^2 = x^2 + 2 x y + y^2, \\
(x - y)^2 = x^2 - 2 x y + y^2.
\end{cases}
\end{align*}
$$

The following expression is an example using a tag and `\bigstar`.

$$
\sqrt{x^2} = +x \lor \sqrt{x^2} = {-x} \tag{$\bigstar$}
$$
